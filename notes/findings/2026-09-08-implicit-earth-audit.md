# Implicit Earth dependences in the founding documents

Measured on 2026-09-08 by reading every decision record from 0004 to 0037, all 128
requirement records in the eleven areas, `docs/practice.md`, `docs/failure-modes.md` and
the whole of `docs/oracles/registry.toml`, in five parallel read-only passes, one per
area group. The question put to each record: where it commits to a law, scheme, test
case, cadence, oracle bar or constant in its standard form, does that form carry a
planet-, star- or composition-dependent quantity that Earth supplied silently, either
folded into a coefficient or omitted because Earth's value is the implicit default?
Every row in the appendix quotes the sentence it judges, so a finding can be checked
against the record without re-reading it.

Severity: high means a non-Earth configuration would silently get an Earth answer or an
ill-defined quantity; medium means wrong only in some configurations (synchronous
rotators, other compositions, other gravities) or fenced on one axis and not another;
low means cosmetic, or already fenced by a stated disposition, lint or oracle.

|---|---|

The records fence the classic leaks well: Coriolis and every rotation-derived quantity,
the day and year as units, Penman-type evaporation, PAR and the photon currency,
Redfield ratios, growing-degree days, the Earth root profile, latitude classifiers and
the dimensional-literal lint are all handled by name. What slips through is subtler and
falls into eleven shapes.

## The shapes of what slipped through

**1. Dimensionless composition ratios evade the literal lint.** REQ-SYS-101 bans a
literal with a physical dimension. The atmosphere's most pervasive Earth numbers are
dimensionless or conventional: the vapour-to-air molar mass ratio, kappa as two
sevenths, gamma as 1.4, the thousand-hectopascal Exner reference. No record names
`M_air`, `R_d`, `cp_d`, `kappa` or `epsilon` as `Derived` from the declared composition,
and ICON's prognostic set carries all of them as literals. The same group is needed by
every land kernel (bulk fluxes, snow metamorphism, soil gas, Medlyn's diffusivity
ratio) and none of the land records says where it comes from. Related and wholly
absent: the transport properties of the gas mixture (viscosity, thermal conductivity,
vapour diffusivity, mean free path), which enter sedimentation, slip correction, Koehler
growth, scalar roughness, snow grain growth and soil aeration.

**2. "Gravity explicit" is asserted for one term and taken to cover the scheme.** The
stream-power coefficient has `rho_w g` written out while Earth runoff and hydraulic
geometry stay folded in the same number, and the law is written in area in 0015 and in
discharge in REQ-HYD-008, where an inherited `g^(1-n)` factor counts gravity twice. The
deposition coefficient hides a settling velocity; hillslope diffusion hides creep under
gravity and climate; glacial erosion has no effective-pressure limb although 0020 claims
the pressure dependence is computed; the volume-area coefficient absorbs the rheology
and balance gradient the model holds. The land hydraulic contract says gravity enters
once, through field capacity, yet stores a conductivity in metres per second that is
Cosby's at Earth gravity and Earth water viscosity. Rothermel's slope factor contains
no gravity at all, so the record's gravity-sweep oracle would find nothing to move.
Kok's dust emission normalises to an Earth air density and an Earth threshold that
are inside the formula as reference values.

**3. "Per composition" is claimed where the sourced data are per Earth air or Earth
seawater.** HITRAN half-widths are air-broadened; the MT_CKD foreign continuum is
water-in-air; the k-table pressure-temperature grid and the RFMIP reference profiles
are Earth's, and the line-by-line oracle shares the same spectroscopy so it cannot fail
on that axis. TEOS-10, the carbonate constants, the freezing point, the brine constants
in Winton's ice and the 0.98 salinity factor in saturation humidity are all fits to
Reference-Composition seawater; the ocean fences salinity, temperature and pressure
carefully and never composition, and `System` declares inventories but no ionic
composition. The honest disposition for these is `Irreducible` with a stated tolerance
and a refusal beyond it, not `Sourced` with a domain the reference cannot deliver.

**4. Oxygen is physics of the declared atmosphere, not biology.** The biology assumption
fences Rubisco kinetics correctly, but the combustion limits and emission factors of
fire, the Bernacchi fit of the compensation point (a direct function of temperature at
21 percent oxygen, so the declared oxygen term never acts), the C4 advantage,
nitrogenase against the nitrogen partial pressure, iron chemistry in an ocean assumed
oxic, and ozone that needs oxygen to exist are all left silent.

**5. Cadences are declared in periods, not durations.** 0008 removed the day as a unit;
the coupled-loop records reintroduce it as a cadence. The daily tier never says which
of the two day functions it steps on, and has no cadence on a synchronous rotator; the
orbital tier gates demography on a period that may be days long; the warm restart is
counted in orbits although the relaxation time is in seconds; the statistics window
reads "the longest cycle period" with the stellar cycle in the eleven-year template and
no rule for zero amplitude; the radiation call interval has no ceiling against eclipses
or the solar day; the epoch rule is undefined at zero obliquity and for an equal binary.

**6. Verification carries the Earth scale the physics shed.** Exit tolerances are in
watts per square metre with no scaling to the configuration's instellation; registry
statistics are in days; terrain bars are in kilometres and Earth empirical bands
registered as FAIL with no Earth-instance scoping, although the registry's own glacier
entry says the terrain is not Earth's; the endorheic bar is a preference; Hack's
exponent is registered as an identity; tier-3 entries do not name the protocol system
they compare against; the M0 gate, the certification case and the per-commit case
validate `Derived` fields on the Earth instance only, so a derivation right on Earth by
coincidence passes; and the registry has no system, clock, star or insolation section
at all, and no tier-1 ocean, sea-ice or land entries though the requirement records
name them.

**7. The solid Earth has no home in `System`.** Thermal subsidence is sourced to an
Earth-fitted plate model; elastic thickness, crustal thickness and the soil column's
basal heat flux (which sets permafrost) need mantle potential temperature, diffusivity,
expansivity and densities that no record declares; the ridge depth follows from the
water inventory through isostasy and is nowhere derived.

**8. Wind speed where stress belongs.** Gas transfer, the wave-slope and whitecap laws,
the sea-salt source, ice drag and the cited bulk transfer coefficients are stated in a
ten-metre wind at Earth air density and gravity. Bracketing the coefficient does not
fix the argument; the general form is friction velocity and gravity.

**9. `Bracketed` written without its mechanisms, and interfaces an Earth dataset can
silently satisfy.** Background diapycnal diffusivity (the ocean's most influential
number, stated only in a consequence line of the moons record that cites the wrong
decision), the ice-strength constants, lead closing, floe size and the bottom-drag
residual carry the word and not the two ends. The tidal dissipation field, the initial
ocean state, the mixed-layer diagnostic and the lead fraction "derived from thickness"
are interfaces that Earth's map, Earth's climatology or an Arctic literal would satisfy.

**10. Record pairs disagree, and the Earth-folded form sits in the decision record.**
The incision law (0015 against REQ-HYD-008), the methane lifetime (0021 bracket against
REQ-BIO-017's derived abundance), the weathering law in the carbon balance (REQ-PED-001
against REQ-PED-011), the lightning proxy, the vertical ladder ("lowest kilometre" in
0005 and 0016 against "scale-height fraction" in the failure-modes review), per-source
against combined k-tables (0016 against 0032), the wilting point and the mortality
cadence. In each case the requirement record already has the generic wording.

**11. The struct's derived functions are complete; its inputs are not.** 0032 derives
eclipses, moonlight and multi-source instellation from the start, but 0004's moon block
has no radius, reflectance, node, periapsis or reference plane; stars have no radius
unless a model is carried; orbits have no body hierarchy; the declared "rotation period"
does not say sidereal; and the deep-atmosphere formulation needs gravity as a function
of radius and latitude with the centrifugal term, where the records name one surface
number.

## The high-severity rows

System layer: the daily tier's day; the vertical ladder's "lowest kilometre" and its
unstated first-guess temperature; the unqualified rotation period; exit tolerances in
Earth units; the moon block's missing radius, reflectance and orbit orientation; the
absent `system.*` registry section.

Atmosphere: the moist thermodynamic group as literals; HITRAN air-broadening; the
MT_CKD partner and range; the k-table pressure-temperature grid outside the content
address; the line-by-line oracle sharing the spectroscopy it should test; the sea-salt
source in ten-metre wind; Kok's Earth reference values.

Ocean: TEOS-10 composition; the carbonate constants and the borate share of alkalinity
from Earth salinity ratios; the unnamed gas-transfer scheme; the background diffusivity.

Terrain: the incision coefficient's folded runoff and the two forms of one law; thermal
subsidence from an Earth plate fit; glacial erosion without the pressure limb.

Land and biosphere: fire without an oxygen window; the methane lifetime contradiction;
Cosby's conductivity at Earth gravity inside a record that claims gravity enters once;
Rothermel's slope factor as the gravity path; Bernacchi's compensation point defeating
the declared oxygen; an Earth phosphorus-yield range registered as FAIL without
Earth-instance scoping.

## What would close them

Stated as the shape of the amendment, not decided here; each touches accepted records.

- One `Derived` group for the properties of the declared gas mixture (molar mass, gas
  constant, heat capacities, kappa, epsilon, viscosity, conductivity, diffusivities,
  mean free path), owned by one component and read by every kernel that needs air; and
  the REQ-SYS-101 lint extended to dimensionless composition ratios and the literal
  list of REQ-PROC-008.
- A per-scheme table of every dimensional constant with its gravity, density and
  viscosity scaling, applying the cryosphere's rule (REQ-CRY-002: state the full
  dimension) everywhere; Earth reference values inside published fits quarantined in
  `EarthRatios`.
- Composition as a declared axis: an ionic composition in the ocean inventory with
  TEOS-10 and the carbonate constants `Irreducible` inside Reference Composition's
  tolerance; per-perturber broadening and continua `Sourced` where measured and
  `Bracketed` where not; the pressure-temperature grid and the profile set for the
  line-by-line references drawn from the configuration's own brackets and hashed.
- An oxygen rule beside the biology assumption: any Earth-measured quantity that
  varies with oxygen partial pressure carries the pressure it was measured at and is
  scaled or refused away from it; fire gets a flammability window.
- Every tier cadence and window as a `Derived` duration in seconds from a named
  relaxation or forcing timescale, rounded to whole periods only where a cycle must
  close, with the undefined limit (synchronous rotation, zero amplitude, zero
  obliquity, equal binary) refused by name.
- Verification made dimensionless or `Derived`: exit tolerances as fractions of
  absorbed instellation, registry statistics in seconds, tier-2 terrain bars scoped to
  the Earth instance, tier-3 entries naming their protocol system as a `Sourced`
  constructor, a `system.*` tier-1 section, tier-1 ocean and land entries, and one
  synthetic non-Earth instance beside `Earth()` in the M0 gate and the certification
  and per-commit cases.
- A solid-Earth thermal block in `System` (mantle potential temperature, diffusivity,
  expansivity, densities, crustal thickness per province) from which subsidence,
  elastic thickness, ridge depth and basal heat flux are `Derived`.
- The disagreeing pairs reconciled to the requirement records' wording, and the
  cross-reference in 0032 corrected to 0017.
- The moon and star blocks of 0004 completed (radius, reflectance spectrum, node,
  periapsis, reference plane; stellar radius; orbit hierarchy; sidereal rotation named
  as the declared quantity), and gravity as a field of radius and latitude.

#### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Cadences are declared in periods, not durations.** The daily tier, the orbital tier, the refresh window, the statistics window and the reconvergence window are all counted in "days", "orbits" or "cycle periods". 0008 removed the day as a *unit*; the coupled-loop records reintroduce it as a *cadence*, and a synchronous rotator, a days-long orbit or a zero-amplitude stellar cycle makes each one undefined. The fix is uniform: every tier and window is a `Derived` duration in seconds from a named relaxation or forcing timescale, rounded to whole periods only where a cycle must close, with the undefined limit refused by name.

2. **The struct's derived functions are complete; the struct's inputs are not.** 0032 derives eclipses, moonlight and multi-source instellation "from the start", but 0004's foundational block lacks the moon radius, reflectance and orbit orientation, the star radius and the multi-body orbit hierarchy those functions read. Because 0007 forbids defaults, these would surface as missing fields at the first configuration with a moon, which is the right failure, but the records should declare them now so the interface really is complete.

3. **Verification units carry the Earth scale the physics has shed.** Exit tolerances, registry statistics in days, terrain cuts in kilometres and the RFMIP profile set all express bars in Earth-magnitude units with no scaling rule, while the physics behind them is generic. Every threshold should be dimensionless or `Derived` from the configuration, with the Earth value printed as the distance to report.

4. **`Derived` fields are proven only where Earth is the answer.** The M0 gate and the short coupled, certification and per-commit cases are named only as `Earth()` or not named at all. A derivation that is right on Earth by coincidence (prograde-only, single-source, spherical, small-e) has no test that can fail. One synthetic non-Earth instance with closed forms, in the registry beside `Earth()`, closes this.

5. **The registry has no system layer.** Every clock, orbit, star and insolation identity the decision records imply is unregistered, so the layer that would leak an Earth default into every component at once is the one layer with no oracle row. Adding the `system.*` section is the single change that turns most of the medium rows above into checked properties.

#### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Dimensionless composition ratios are the gap in the fence.** REQ-SYS-101 bans dimensional literals; the atmosphere's most pervasive Earth literals (`0.622`, `0.608`, `2/7`, `1.4`, `1000 hPa` as a ratio) are dimensionless or conventional, and no atmosphere record names `M_air`, `R_d`, `cp_d`, `kappa`, `epsilon` as `Derived` from the declared composition. Row 1 alone touches the core's prognostic variable, `q_sat`, buoyancy, CAPE, dry static energy and the Courant ceiling.

2. **"Per composition" is claimed where the sourced data are per air.** HITRAN air-broadening, MT_CKD's foreign continuum, Kok's `rho_a0`-standardised threshold, the sea-salt `U10` fits and the deposition velocities are all fitted with N2/O2 air at 1 bar as an unstated perturber or normaliser. Rows 2, 3, 6, 7 and 18 are the same shape: the record declares the dependence explicit and the named reference cannot carry it. The LBL oracle (row 5) shares the same inputs, so it cannot catch this class; the record should say what the oracle does and does not validate.

3. **The gas mixture's transport properties are nowhere.** Viscosity, thermal conductivity, vapour diffusivity and mean free path enter sedimentation, slip correction, Koehler growth, scalar roughness and snow metamorphism; every record reaches for "air density" and stops. One `Derived` group (row 9) closes rows 9, 10, 18, 19 and 22 together.

4. **The condensable is water by silence.** No atmosphere record declares the condensable species, yet every phase-change, optics and activation relation is water's. Declaring it (row 8) is cheap and makes the absence of any other condensable a priced absence rather than an assumption.

5. **The "one place" claim is an overclaim that will hide the rest.** 0013: "The convective parameterisation is the one place the atmosphere carries irreducible Earth-fitted numbers"; 0016: "The convection scheme is the one place the atmosphere carries Earth-fitted numbers, and both are brackets to sweep." The same record brackets autoconversion, the dispersion parameter, orographic efficiency and the closure width, and rows 2, 3, 6, 7, 12, 15, 18, 21 and 22 add more. The sentence should be replaced by a pointer to the registry's list of `Bracketed` and `Irreducible` atmosphere constants, so the count reviewed at every milestone (0007) is the authority rather than a prose claim.

One further note outside the strict area: the psychrometric-constant class (Penman, Priestley-Taylor) is explicitly excluded by `docs/decisions/0018-one-land-column.md` ("Penman or Priestley-Taylor evaporation ... Rejected as a second [definition]"), so it is fenced and does not appear in the table.

#### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Composition is the missing axis.** Every seawater property the records name (EOS, freezing point, heat capacity, vapour pressure, viscosity and molecular diffusivities, carbonate equilibria, the borate share of alkalinity, sea-ice brine constants, evaporite saturation) is a fit to Reference-Composition seawater. The records fence S, T, p and g carefully and never composition, and the `System` struct has "inventories" but no record declares an ionic composition for the ocean. The named reference formulations (TEOS-10, Lueker/Dickson/Mucci constants, Winton's brine terms) cannot be re-derived for another solute, so the honest wording is an `Irreducible` composition fixed to Reference Composition with a stated tolerance and a refusal beyond it, not a `Sourced` law with a domain. Rows 1, 2, 7, 11, 12, 14, 18, 26.

2. **Wind speed where stress belongs.** Gas transfer, Cox-Munk wave slope, whitecaps, ice drag and the cited Large-Yeager transfer coefficients are all stated in U10 at Earth air density and gravity. Bracketing the coefficient does not fix the argument: the general form is u* (or the stress the atmosphere already writes) and g. Rows 3, 11, 15, 16.

3. **`Bracketed` used as a fence without naming the bracket's mechanisms.** The disposition's own rule (both mechanisms named, sweep declared) is what would expose an Earth-observed bracket end: background diffusivity, P*, lead-closing thickness, floe size, bottom-drag residual velocity. Several records write the word `Bracketed` and stop. Rows 4, 9, 16, 21, and the "derived from thickness" claim in row 9 is the same problem inverted.

4. **Solar-spectrum fractions buried inside non-radiation schemes.** The ecosystem's PAR path is fenced; the sea-ice penetrating fraction and extinction are not. Any "fraction of shortwave that ..." term outside the radiation module needs to be a per-band `Derived` quantity from the radiation's band structure. Row 8 (and by analogy the ocean column's shortwave deposition, which REQ-OCN-007 does fence).

5. **Interfaces that an Earth dataset can silently satisfy.** The tidal dissipation field "on the bathymetry", the initial ocean state, the MLD diagnostic and the oxic-ocean iron chemistry each have an interface or a ledger but no rule saying the content is `Derived` from the declared system rather than loaded or assumed. Rows 5, 10, 13, 27. Related: D32's consequence cites "the ocean scope (decision 0016)" where 0017 is meant, and it is the only place the interior mixing bracket is stated at all.

#### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **"g explicit" is treated as sufficient when the coefficient also hides climate.** The stream-power `K`, the deposition coefficient `G`, hillslope `D`, glacial `K_g` and the Bahr coefficient each have gravity called out (or bracketed) while Earth's runoff, balance gradient or precipitation stays folded into the same number. The records' own remedy (REQ-CRY-002: state the coefficient's full dimension) is applied in the cryosphere and not in the terrain rows.

2. **Two forms of one law across records.** Incision is `K A^m S^n` in 0015 and `k_e Q^m S^n` in REQ-HYD-008; the latter adds a `g^(1-n)` factor inherited from the predecessor's dimensionless slope that double-counts gravity in the SI form. The "one definition per quantity" rule is violated at the one law gravity was supposed to enter through.

3. **Solid-Earth thermal state has no home in `System`.** Thermal subsidence, elastic thickness, crustal thickness and the soil column's basal heat flux all need mantle potential temperature, diffusivity and crust thicknesses; every record marks them `Sourced` (Earth) or "computed" without a rule. This is the largest untouched implicit-Earth block in the area, and it sets bathymetry, flexural wavelength and permafrost for every configuration.

4. **Tier-2 terrain bars are not scoped to the Earth instance.** Hypsometry (absolute kilometres), concavity, drainage density, endorheic share and denudation are registered as FAIL bars with no "Earth constructor only" clause, and REQ-TER-016 and 0015 make them the M1 gate on "the hypsometry the climate tiles read". Since 0015 rejects relief rescaling by `g`, the only consistent reading is FAIL on the Earth instance and REPORT elsewhere, and the registry should say it.

5. **Water properties are named at one temperature.** `rho_w` and `mu` appear as constants inside `K = k rho g / mu`, the incision `K`, settling velocity and `z0`; no terrain or hydrology record says they are evaluated from the water EOS at the column's temperature (the ocean record REQ-OCN-003 apparently does). Warm or cold configurations shift transmissivity by a factor of several silently.

#### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Oxygen is treated as biology when it is atmosphere.** The records fence Rubisco kinetics as `Irreducible`, and REQ-BIO-007 and REQ-BIO-011 correctly read O2 from B2, but three physical O2 dependences slip through the fence: combustion limits and emission factors (rows 1, 22), the Gamma* fit that Bernacchi supplies at 21 percent O2 (row 5), and the C4 advantage (row 15). The "declared composition" clause needs a companion rule: any Earth-measured quantity that varies with pO2 carries its measurement pO2 and is scaled or refused away from it.

2. **"g explicit" is asserted for one term of a scheme and taken to cover the scheme.** REQ-PED-007 (field capacity, but not Ks), 0018 snow (overburden, but not metamorphism), 0018 dust (threshold, but not the emission flux), and REQ-BIO-015 (a slope term that in Rothermel contains no g at all) share the shape. The cure is a per-scheme table of every dimensional constant with its g, rho_a and mu scaling, rather than a sentence naming one.

3. **Gas properties of the declared atmosphere have no owner in the land records.** rho_a, c_p, M, epsilon, binary diffusivities (H2O:CO2 for Medlyn, O2 in soil, vapour in snow pores) and energy per flash are each needed by a land or biosphere kernel and none of the audited records says where they come from (rows 9, 11, 12, 17, 27, 30). If an atmosphere record already derives them, the land records should cite it; if not, a short "gas properties `Derived` from `System`" requirement is missing.

4. **Two records disagree about which formulation drives a solved quantity, and the Earth-fitted one is the fallback.** CH4 lifetime (0021 bracket vs REQ-BIO-017 chemistry loop, row 2), weathering law in the pCO2 balance (REQ-PED-001 kinetic vs REQ-PED-011 published-form bracket, row 14), and the lightning proxy (row 26). In each case the decision record carries the older, Earth-folded form and the requirement record the derived one; the decision records should be brought to the requirement records' wording.

5. **The time-base and Earth-target disciplines are stated for the biosphere and not extended to pedology.** REQ-BIO-001's classes and REQ-BIO-005's refusal of Earth intervals cover bio records well, but the ped records quote thresholds per Earth year and per Earth month (rows 20, 38) and one carries an Earth-fitted FAIL bar with no Earth-instance restriction (row 6). A one-line cross-reference from the ped README to REQ-BIO-001, plus `tier = 2` on the phosphorus-yield bar, closes both.

One structural note outside any single row: the tiering of B9 (fast column, "daily" growth on the planet's own day, orbital establishment, slow statistics) presumes a rotation short compared with phenological time and an orbit long compared with a rotation. A synchronous rotator or a very slow rotator collapses two tiers into one; none of the audited records says what the daily tier means there. That is a design gap rather than an Earth-folded constant, so it is not in the table.
