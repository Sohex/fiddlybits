# Thermodynamics.jl

**What it is.** A moist-thermodynamics library: saturation vapour pressure,
saturation specific humidity, the moist gas constant and heat capacities,
virtual and potential temperatures, moist static and internal energies, and a
saturation-adjustment root solve. It holds no state, no grid and no arrays; every
exported function is a pointwise `@inline` taking a parameter set and scalars.
It is the package REQ-ATM-017 is closest to in shape, which is why it is
surveyed first.

**Read against.** Commit `9f68819e4e299a38f9308ec1599dbc1fb52af4cb` on `main`,
`Project.toml` version 1.3.0, read 2026-09-09. Files read in full or in the
relevant part: `src/Parameters.jl`, `src/ThermoTypes.jl`, `src/Thermodynamics.jl`,
`src/air_properties.jl`, `src/air_humidities.jl`, `src/air_temperatures.jl`,
`src/air_saturation_functions.jl`, `src/saturation_adjustment.jl`,
`src/TemperatureProfiles.jl`, `src/DataCollection.jl`, `src/aux_functions.jl`,
`ext/CreateParametersExt.jl`, `test/test_common.jl`, `test/correctness.jl`,
`test/physical_properties.jl`, `docs/src/index.md`.

**What of it would be used.** Nothing is adopted by this survey. The candidate
surface, if a later decision adopted it, is the saturation and mixture relations
of `air_saturation_functions.jl` and `air_properties.jl`, and the
`ThermodynamicsParameters` shape as a model for the atmosphere's property group.

## The generalisation question, answered against the source

**Every constant is a field; none is baked into a relation.** The parameter set is
one flat keyword-only struct, `ThermodynamicsParameters{FT} <:
AbstractThermodynamicsParameters{FT}` in `src/Parameters.jl`, with 26 fields
including `T_0`, `T_triple`, `T_freeze`, `T_icenuc`, `press_triple`, `R_d`, `R_v`,
`cp_d`, `cp_v`, `cp_l`, `cp_i`, `LH_v0`, `LH_s0`, `p_ref_theta`, `MSLP` and
`grav`. The relations read them only through accessor functions generated by a
loop over `fieldnames(ThermodynamicsParameters)`, so there is no second path to a
value. A grep of the whole of `src/` for numeric literals returns fewer than ten
sites, and none of them is a gas property.

**The molar-mass ratio is derived from the two gas constants, not written down.**
There is no `epsilon` field and no literal ratio anywhere. `Parameters.jl`
defines `Rv_over_Rd(ps) = R_v(ps) / R_d(ps)`, and the saturation humidity and the
partial pressures are written in it, for example in `air_humidities.jl`:

    return p * (1 - q_tot) / (1 - q_tot + q_vap * Rv_over_Rd)

The virtual temperature is likewise the ratio of mixture to bulk gas constant
rather than an air-specific expansion, in `air_temperatures.jl`:

    @inline function virtual_temperature(param_set::APS, T, q_tot=0, q_liq=0, q_ice=0)
        R_d = TP.R_d(param_set)
        R_m = gas_constant_air(param_set, q_tot, q_liq, q_ice)
        return T * R_m / R_d
    end

This is the form REQ-ATM-017 asks for, and it is the strongest thing in the
package: the composition ratio the implicit-Earth audit found as a number in
every atmosphere record is here a function of two supplied fields.

**Saturation vapour pressure is a Clausius-Clapeyron integration over the
supplied heat capacities, not a fitted water curve.** This is the decisive
finding. `saturation_vapor_pressure(param_set, T, ::Liquid)` passes `LH_v0` and
`cp_v - cp_l` to a single internal routine, and the ice branch passes `LH_s0`
and `cp_v - cp_i`. The routine, `_saturation_vapor_pressure_calc` in
`src/air_saturation_functions.jl`, evaluates

    p_sat = press_triple * exp( (Delta_cp / R_v) * log(T / T_triple)
                                + (LH_0 - Delta_cp * T_0) / R_v
                                  * (1 / T_triple - 1 / T) )

which is the Rankine-Kirchhoff form: the exact integral of Clausius-Clapeyron
under constant specific heats, anchored at the triple point of whatever species
`press_triple` and `T_triple` describe. There is no Tetens, Buck, Bolton or
Wexler polynomial in the tree, and no tabulated water curve. Supply another
species' triple point, gas constant, vapour and condensed heat capacities and
reference latent heats and the same code returns that species' curve. The
formulation's own consistency is pinned upstream by an identity test:
`test/physical_properties.jl` shifts `T_0` by a constant and shifts `LH_v0` and
`LH_s0` along their Kirchhoff lines, and asserts the saturation vapour pressure
and the recovered temperature are unchanged.

**The mixture rule is mass-fraction over exactly two gases.**
`gas_constant_air` in `air_properties.jl` returns `R_d * (1 - q_tot) + R_v *
q_vap`, and `cp_m` mixes `cp_d`, `cp_v`, `cp_l` and `cp_i` the same way. The
bulk gas is one lumped species with a single `R_d` and `cp_d`.

**So: a CO2-dominated atmosphere with CO2 as the condensable, or an H2-He
mixture with water as the condensable, gets correct relations without a fork,
inside three limits that this project would hit.**

1. *One condensable, two condensed phases.* `Phase` has exactly two subtypes,
   `Liquid` and `Ice` (`src/ThermoTypes.jl`), the state variables are
   `(q_tot, q_liq, q_ice)` throughout, and the phase partition is a single
   `liquid_fraction`. A configuration with two condensables, or with a
   condensable that also makes up part of the bulk (CO2 condensing out of a CO2
   atmosphere, which changes `R_d` where it condenses), is not representable.
   The bulk gas constant is a scalar parameter, not a field, so no spatial
   variation of the bulk composition can be carried at all.
2. *Calorically perfect gas.* `cp_m`'s own docstring says "The specific heat
   capacities are assumed to be constant (calorically perfect air)", and
   `docs/src/index.md` says the same of the whole formulation. REQ-ATM-017 item 2
   requires per-gas heat capacities with temperature dependence from the standard
   thermochemical tables. For air over a terrestrial range the difference is
   small; for CO2 or H2 over the range a general configuration reaches it is not,
   and the error propagates into the saturation curve, which inherits the
   constant `Delta_cp`.
3. *Thermodynamics only.* The package has no transport properties. Viscosity,
   thermal conductivity, binary diffusivity and mean free path, which are more
   than half of REQ-ATM-017's property group and the members the audit found in
   five places and sourced in none, are simply absent. Whatever this project does
   about them, it does alone.

**A declared capability with no demonstration.** `docs/src/index.md` claims
"Extensible parameters: Easily adapt to different planetary atmospheres via
`ClimaParams`". No test in the tree constructs a parameter set with a different
gas. The two tests that build a modified parameter set,
`test/correctness.jl` line 420 and `test/physical_properties.jl` line 251, vary
only `T_0` and the reference latent heats along their Kirchhoff lines. Both
reference parameter sets in `test/test_common.jl` are
`TP.ThermodynamicsParameters(Float64)` and `(Float32)`, which is the ClimaParams
Earth default set. The generality is in the code and is untested; the claim is
declared, not demonstrated (C3).

## Assumptions it carries

**Earth defaults.** Present, and not in the relations but at the door. The
ClimaParams extension `ext/CreateParametersExt.jl` defines
`ThermodynamicsParameters(::Type{FT}) = ThermodynamicsParameters(CP.create_toml_dict(FT))`,
so a caller who writes `ThermodynamicsParameters(Float64)` and names nothing
receives all 26 fields from ClimaParams' shipped `parameters.toml` with no
declaration and no warning. That is exactly the pattern decision 0007 refuses:
a default is an assumption that nothing declares. The direct keyword constructor
is `Base.@kwdef` with no default on any field, so the struct itself refuses a
missing field; the leak is entirely in the convenience method.

**Planetary constants inside a thermodynamics parameter set.** `grav` and `MSLP`
are fields of `ThermodynamicsParameters` (A2). Reading every use site (C1):
neither appears anywhere in `air_properties.jl`, `air_humidities.jl`,
`air_energies.jl`, `air_temperatures.jl`, `air_pressure_and_density.jl`,
`air_saturation_functions.jl` or `saturation_adjustment.jl`. They bind only in
`src/TemperatureProfiles.jl`, an initialisation and test utility, which computes
a dry adiabatic lapse rate `Gamma = grav / cp_d` and a hydrostatic pressure from
`MSLP`. That is a constant gravity, not `g(r, phi)`, but it is confined to a
utility this project would not use. Recording it as a clean separation: the
relations carry no planetary constant.

**Calendar or time representation.** Clean negative. `Dates` is not imported
anywhere in `src/`, there is no clock, no day, no year and no epoch. The package
is time-independent.

**Grid or mesh.** Clean negative. No array, no grid type, no topology, no
horizontal or vertical index anywhere in `src/`. Every function is pointwise on
scalars and composes under broadcast; `Base.broadcastable` is defined on the
parameter set and on the dispatch singletons so they ride along as scalars.

**Index base.** Clean negative, and vacuously so: there is no array in the used
surface to index.

**Precision.** `FT` is a type parameter of the parameter set and every relation
promotes to the widest of its arguments, deliberately, so a `Float64` input
against a `Float32` parameter set widens rather than truncates
(`liquid_fraction`'s comment says so explicitly). The test suite runs `Float32`
and `Float64`. Two numerics floors are precision-dependent and hard-coded:
`epsilon_numerics(FT) = sqrt(floatmin(FT))` and `T_positive_floor(FT) =
sqrt(eps(FT))` in `src/aux_functions.jl`, both guards against `log` of a
non-positive temperature inside a GPU kernel rather than physical limits, and the
comment says so.

**Threading and GPU model.** Clean negative for the relations: they are pure
`@inline` scalar functions with no allocation, no branching on a
`DomainError`, and `ifelse` in place of branches at the guards, which is the
right shape for a kernel. `test/runtests_gpu.jl` exercises them on a device.
Not clean for the diagnostics: see mutable global state.

**Mutable global state.** Present, in `src/DataCollection.jl`. Five module-level
`const ... = Ref{Int}(0)` counters accumulate saturation-adjustment solver
statistics, and the collection is switched on by the caller redefining a method,
`TD.solution_type() = RS.VerboseSolution()`, at global scope. The module's own
docstring warns it "was designed for unthreaded single processor runs, and may
not work correctly for threaded / multi-processor runs". It is inert on the
default `CompactSolution` path, where `log_meta` is a no-op returning `nothing`,
so it does not contaminate a normal run; but it is a global mutable and a
method-redefinition switch in a package otherwise free of both.

**Clamps, floors and limiters (B5).** The Newton step in
`saturation_adjustment.jl` is clamped by a hard-coded step limiter set to
`FT(50)`, a 50 K maximum temperature increment per iteration, and then floored at
`T / 2`. The no-condensate phase partition ramps linearly over a hard-coded
`FT(0.1)` K either side of `T_freeze`, a smoothing width in kelvin with no
parameter behind it. `liquid_fraction_ramp` clamps its argument to `[0, 1]` before exponentiation
because a negative base raised to `pow_icenuc` throws in a kernel. The search is
bounded by `T_min` and `T_max` fields, whose shipped values are 1 K and 1000 K.

**Fail-open branches (C4).** The convenience signature
`saturation_adjustment(param_set, indep_vars, var1, var2, q_tot; maxiter::Int =
2)` runs a fixed two iterations by default and returns `(; T, q_liq, q_ice)`
with the convergence flag dropped; the docstring says "Use the full signature if
you need the `converged` flag", and its own worked example notes the returned
temperature is about 0.4 K from the converged answer. Nothing refuses. The
longer signature does return `converged`, but a caller has to ask for it. This
project's requirement is a refusal, not a flag a caller may ignore.

**Second constant sets (C5).** Clean negative inside the package: every constant
has exactly one home, the parameter struct, and every derived combination
(`kappa_d`, `cv_d`, `cv_v`, `LH_f0`, `e_int_v0`, `Rv_over_Rd`) is a function of
fields rather than a stored number. The duplication is upstream in ClimaParams;
see that record.

**Comment against value (B4).** No mismatch found. The docstrings on
`saturation_vapor_pressure_calc`, `cp_m` and `liquid_fraction` each state the
approximation being made, and each matches the code.

## Verdict

**Borrow ideas only.** The relations generalise further than expected, and the
parameter-struct shape with every ratio derived from supplied fields is the form
REQ-ATM-017 asks for and should be copied; but the package covers only the
thermodynamic half of this project's property group, assumes one condensable in
one lumped bulk gas with constant heat capacities, and reaches this project's
disposition system nowhere, so adopting it would mean owning a fork of exactly
the half we would still have to extend.

The ideas worth carrying, named so a later decision can cite them: the
Rankine-Kirchhoff saturation curve as a Clausius-Clapeyron integration over
supplied heat capacities, which is how a condensable's saturation vapour
pressure generalises off Earth and is the shape REQ-ATM-017 item 6's
Clausius-Clapeyron residual oracle should check against; deriving the
molar-mass ratio as `R_v / R_d` rather than storing an `epsilon`, which deletes
one of the dimensionless literals REQ-ATM-017 item 4 bans; the
reference-temperature invariance identity of `test/physical_properties.jl` as a
tier 1 oracle this project should own for its own energy definitions; and
`Base.@kwdef` with no default on any field, which is already decision 0007's
constructor rule and is worth noting as independently arrived at.

No adopt recommendation is made, so no named test and no enabling decision is
required here. Were a later decision to propose adoption anyway, the assumption
that would have to be caught is the ClimaParams-defaulted convenience
constructor, and the test that would catch it is the same non-Earth instance
oracle REQ-ATM-017 item 6 already requires: the carbon-dioxide-bulk and
hydrogen-helium-bulk property instances, which a parameter set silently filled
from Earth defaults cannot reproduce.
