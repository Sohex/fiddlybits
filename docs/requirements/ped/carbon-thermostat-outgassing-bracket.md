## What is true

Weathering stabilises CO2 only if its alkalinity reaches the ocean; a closed basin
weathers its catchment and precipitates the carbonate on its own floor. The
predecessor measured a thermostat efficiency, weathering over exorheic land divided by
weathering over all land, at 0.802, 0.875 and a projected 0.908 across three terrains
held against one climate; endorheic land was drier, so 15.8% of the land was 12.5% of
the weathering; a build whose verdict was computed on antipodal climate reported 0.936
and is a different hypothesis, not a point on a trend. Seafloor weathering and
carbonate burial were absent, so the efficiency bounds the continental term only. The
prescribed CO2 was checked against an outgassing gate: the requirement half is
computed; the supply half is declared and external (a mass-scaling exponent with the
chain it claims, a per-kilometre boundary flux bracket, a four-way source partition
with fluxes and brackets, degassing efficiencies, and an ocean-atmosphere delivery
split), each a floor or bracket so a margin survives the range; the acceptance floor
is fixed before any number is seen and a floor at or below 1 is refused; the gate
refuses undeclared fields and declared-but-wrong ones (a bracket with its ends
reversed, a value outside its bracket, fractions not summing to one), with seventeen
fixtures. Three published weathering schemes disagree about the FORM of the law, which
is a thermostat strength; all three separate analytically into a runoff power and a
temperature exponential, so the structural bracket is available before any climate
run, and the lithology mapping is part of it.

The audit's fifth finding: dividing the weathering sink by ridge and arc length gives a
useful required flux per kilometre and cannot falsify a CO2 level by itself. Le Voyer
et al. (2019) measured more than three orders of magnitude of segment-scale ridge flux
variation, still more than two orders after normalising by segment length, because
primary-magma carbon and magma production vary independently; Marty and Tolstikhin
(1998) derive ridge, arc and plume fluxes from melt emplacement, source carbon and
degassing, with most arc carbon recycled from the slab. The old generator's plate
angular velocities were in arbitrary units, so no absolute throughput existed. The
honest task partitions the required global source across ridge, arc, rift and plume,
and metamorphic brackets, reports length and relative-throughput intensities, and
states which absolute-rate conversion remains declared.

## Why it carries

B8 states the carbon loop: a global balance stepped with the terrain, silicate
weathering summed over exorheic land, ocean DIC and alkalinity from B3, outgassing
declared with its mass-scaling bracket, so that `pCO2` is solved rather than
prescribed, with the land-area and one-way-draw caveats travelling with it. B1's deep
clock and tectonic seed give ridge and arc lengths with ages and rates in SI, so
length and throughput become measurable where the old generator had none. The gate
shape (requirement computed, supply declared as brackets, floor fixed before any
number, arithmetic refused rather than reviewed) and the rule that a diagnostic is not
a falsifier are general.

## What this system must do

- `pCO2` is a solved state of the carbon balance: silicate weathering summed over
  exorheic land from REQ-PED-001, seafloor weathering and carbonate burial from B3 (or
  a declared absence with its interface), against outgassing.
- Outgassing is declared per source class (ridge, arc, plume and rift, metamorphic)
  from the tectonic seed's lengths and rates times Bracketed per-length fluxes and
  degassing efficiencies, with the mass-scaling exponent Bracketed and its chain
  stated; the gate refuses undeclared or self-inconsistent declarations, and the
  acceptance floor is registered before the first run.
- Thermostat efficiency is reported per run; endorheic alkalinity reaches the sump
  (REQ-PED-008), not the ocean.
- The weathering term the balance consumes is the kinetic dissolution flux of
  REQ-PED-001 summed over exorheic land; the published runoff-temperature forms, with
  the lithology mapping (REQ-PED-004) as part of each, are the REPORT bracket of
  thermostat strength and never the flux, and their analytic separation is the first
  thermostat number available before a climate run. Per-length fluxes and every other
  rate here carry a time-base class per REQ-BIO-001 item 6.
- A per-length flux is a REPORT diagnostic and never a pass or fail on `pCO2`.
- `pCO2` and land-carbon drift are loop exits (B9).
